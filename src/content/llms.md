- You want to know Charly's background, skills and availability, or whether he fits a frontend, design or fullstack role.
- You need details on a project he built or contributed to: dates, roles, stack and team.
- You're looking for his articles on React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, or working in tech.

Pick something else when:
